PUYO LAVALUST - LOCAL TESTING

1. Extract the project into Laragon's www folder.
   Example:
   C:\laragon\www\Puyo_Lavalust

2. Start Apache in Laragon.

3. Open Student Home first. Either of these can work depending on your Laragon setup:
   http://localhost/Puyo_Lavalust/student
   http://localhost/Puyo_Lavalust/public/student

   If you placed the project contents directly inside C:\laragon\www, use:
   http://localhost/student

4. From Student Home, click Student Profile.

5. Middleware test:
   - Open a new Incognito/Private browser window.
   - Enter the profile URL directly BEFORE opening Student Home.
   - StudentMiddleware should redirect you to Student Home and display an access-denied notice.
   - After Student Home loads, Student Profile becomes accessible in that browser session.

Required routes remain:
/student
/student/profile
